Mentored an MSDS capstone research team (HU Huanjia, LI Yifei, WEI Jianghong, SUN Qixuan, SHAO Jiayu) on **TravelAgent**, an LLM-based travel planning agent. I guided the team through problem formulation, method design, experiments, and academic writing.

The team received the **MSDS Outstanding Performance Award 2026** from the Department of Data Science, City University of Hong Kong.
